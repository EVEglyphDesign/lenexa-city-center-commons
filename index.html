Mood is drawn from note tags, in your browser, at page load. A tile with no notes renders hopeful — light, unfinished, waiting. Absence of data is not a signal.

No accounts. No analytics. Nothing here is reported to the city, the county, or anyone else — see the canon.

Publish-only. This surface observes and publishes. It does not notify Lenexa PD, code enforcement, KDHE, OSHA, or any other authority — ever. If you want an authority involved, the third door lists the real intake channels and you file it yourself. In an emergency, call 911; this map is not an emergency channel.

No landlord field. No management field. No ranking of one place against another. No accounts, no cookies, no analytics. Public repo, MIT licensed, built to be forked.
